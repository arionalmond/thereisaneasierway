How to play

Clear the board by removing matching pairs of tiles. Every deal can be cleared.

  • You can only pick a free tile: nothing sits on top of it, and its left or right side is open.
  • Tiles match when they are identical. Any flower (梅 蘭 菊 竹) matches any other flower, and any season (春 夏 秋 冬) matches any other season.
  • Stuck? Undo a few moves, or Shuffle the remaining tiles.
  • Keys: N new game, U undo, H hint, S shuffle, Esc clear the selection. Tab moves between free tiles.

Your game in progress and your best time are saved in this browser.
